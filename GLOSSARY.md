# Turner's Decks

The public website for Turner's Decks and the CRM the owner uses to follow up on the people it brings in.

## Language

**Lead**:
The record of one inquiry from the website or entered by hand, from first submission until it is won or lost.
_Avoid_: Contact, customer, prospect

**Homeowner**:
The person a Lead is about: the one who asked about a deck.
_Avoid_: Contact, customer, client, user

**Owner alert**:
The email the owner gets about every new Lead.
_Avoid_: Notification, lead email

**Estimate email**:
The email a Homeowner gets after using the estimator, with their price range.
_Avoid_: Confirmation, acknowledgement, quote email

## Estimator

**Estimator settings**:
The owner's editable draft of the estimator's prices, questions, options and disclaimers.
_Avoid_: Price list, config, form builder

**Published version**:
A numbered, unchangeable copy of the Estimator settings that the public estimator uses; a new one is made each time the
owner publishes.
_Avoid_: Release, revision

**Estimate snapshot**:
What a Lead keeps from the estimator: the Published version number, the questions and options shown, the Homeowner's
answers, and the resulting price range.
_Avoid_: Quote (a Quote is a formal proposal)
